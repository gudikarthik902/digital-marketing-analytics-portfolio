# B2B Website and GA4 Lead-Generation Measurement

This case study demonstrates how Crown Industries could measure and improve a B2B packaging-machinery lead-generation funnel using a GA4-style event framework and synthetic data.

## Business objective

Create a practical measurement system that connects acquisition channels and landing-page engagement to specification downloads, form starts, qualified leads, and quote requests.

## Analysis included

- Acquisition-channel and landing-page performance
- Lead-generation funnel conversion and drop-off
- Product engagement and specification-download behavior
- Lead and quote-request measurement
- GA4 event taxonomy with key-event definitions
- Event ownership and quality-assurance methods

## Workbook structure

- **Dashboard:** Summary KPIs and performance visuals
- **Funnel Analysis:** Stage-by-stage conversion and drop-off
- **Web Data:** 520 synthetic weekly channel and landing-page records
- **Event Plan:** Nine-event tracking specification, ownership, and QA
- **Methodology:** Definitions, assumptions, and analytical approach

## Tools and skills demonstrated

GA4 measurement planning, funnel analysis, conversion-rate analysis, channel analysis, landing-page analysis, event taxonomy design, data quality assurance, Excel formulas, and dashboard design.
