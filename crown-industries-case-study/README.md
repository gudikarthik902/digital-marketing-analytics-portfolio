# Crown Industries — U.S. Packaging Machinery Marketing Analytics

> Portfolio simulation using synthetic data. This project does not contain actual customer, campaign, financial, or confidential Crown Industries information.

## Business scenario

Crown Industries is modeled as a B2B packaging-machinery manufacturer entering the U.S. market. The marketing team needs to generate qualified demand for pouch fillers, cartoning machines, labeling systems, and case packers while showing how campaign investment contributes to pipeline and attributed revenue.

## Project objective

Build an executive-ready measurement framework that answers:

- Which channels generate the most leads and sales opportunities?
- Where does the buyer journey lose prospects?
- Which products and regions deserve additional campaign investment?
- How should budget be reallocated to improve lead quality and revenue efficiency?

## Dataset

The synthetic dataset contains 480 campaign records covering January–June 2026 across:

- Channels: Google Search, LinkedIn, email, trade media, and retargeting
- Regions: Northeast, Midwest, South, and West
- Products: pouch fillers, cartoning machines, labeling systems, and case packers
- Funnel stages: impressions, clicks, leads, MQLs, SQLs, opportunities, wins, pipeline, and attributed revenue

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| CTR | Clicks ÷ Impressions |
| Lead conversion rate | Leads ÷ Clicks |
| CPL | Spend ÷ Leads |
| ROAS | Attributed revenue ÷ Spend |
| Win rate | Wins ÷ Opportunities |

## Tools demonstrated

- Excel dashboarding and formula-driven KPI reporting
- Campaign and channel performance analysis
- Lead-funnel and customer-journey analysis
- Regional and product segmentation
- Executive reporting and optimization recommendations
- Data hygiene, documentation, and reproducible synthetic data

## Portfolio findings

1. Google Search generates the highest lead volume and should remain the core intent-capture channel.
2. Retargeting strengthens conversion efficiency by re-engaging known prospects.
3. Email creates strong revenue efficiency because it nurtures previously acquired contacts at relatively low media cost.
4. LinkedIn remains useful for enterprise audience reach and high-value account targeting, even when immediate ROAS is lower.
5. Product-specific landing pages, ROI calculators, technical videos, and specification downloads can improve progression from MQL to SQL.
